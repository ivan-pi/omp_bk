p = 1..6 (padded): the fast index padded to 4 or 8, every contraction a SIMD16 mad.
EPT (elements per thread, next element prefetched): helps at p = 1 (12.9 -> 16.7 at
EPT = 8) and p = 3 (+1-2%), hurts from p = 4 (registers; p = 6 spills at EPT >= 2).
Scattered stores of 32 instead of 16 lanes (odd nm): no change.

p = 7, 8 (split): indices of extent 9, 10 stored as 8 + tail blocks, exact size.
  p = 7, steps: padded-to-nq regions 9.9 GDoF/s (compiler-split rows, 5900 movs, spills)
    -> split blocks with a SIMD8 mad per row, tails by columns: 28.5
  p = 8: one thread per element keeps ~2000 floats live and spills 4.6 KB: 7.5 GDoF/s;
    a pair of threads per element (p split 4+1 per thread, partial out added through
    SLM): 16.4; plus no runtime calls of the constexpr block-offset function (an
    offset used outside a constexpr context was compiled as a subroutine call and
    indirect addressing): 26.6-27.0; three handoff rounds through a 1 KB SLM slot
    instead of one 2.9 KB slot: best at wg 4-8 (28.1), one round best at wg 16.
